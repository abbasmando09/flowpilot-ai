Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FlowPilot AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you submit through our website.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a property enquiry, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property type
  • Buying, renting or selling preferences
  • Information included in your message

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to property enquiries.
  • Connect customers with relevant property services.
  • Manage and organize customer enquiries.
  • Analyze enquiries using AI-assisted tools.
  • Improve our customer service.

4. Data Storage and AI Processing

Enquiry information is stored in a Google Sheets database. AI-assisted analysis may be performed using Google's Gemini services to summarize enquiries and classify potential leads.

Information is processed by these services according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Information Sharing

Information may be accessible to authorized personnel responsible for managing enquiries and to service providers involved in operating the website and its automation features.

6. Data Security

We use access controls and reasonable security measures to help protect personal information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may contact the website operator to make a request.

9. Contact Information

For privacy questions or requests, contact the website operator using the contact details provided on the website.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
